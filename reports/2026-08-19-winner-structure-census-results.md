Every single one of our 64,098 registered candidates and 20,320 selected
lineups carries the full QB-double-stack-plus-bring-back shape and puts
4+ players in one game (the mandate makes 4 mechanical: QB + 2 catchers
+ bring-back). The winners' MODAL shape is a single stack partner, no
bring-back, spread across ~6 games. The construction mandates confine
100% of our volume to a structural region containing 16% of actual
winners — a sharper statement of the exclusion than the 43/51 legality
count, because it shows the winners' mass sits at shapes we cannot even
approach, not just outside a rule boundary.

Tension worth naming: the dependence remeasurement says the law
UNDER-couples QB–WR, yet construction FORCES two QB partners into every
lineup. We mandate structurally what the law does not reward, while the
field wins mostly with less stacking and more game spread.

## A12 — the eight rule-legal winners: the blocker is worlds, not players

For the 8 winners satisfying every production rule: **7 of 8 have zero
players missing from our pool** (one misses a single player); their
best generating worlds rank **43–119** — every one past the top-40
solve horizon; and even in those worlds the law's optimum sits 17–57
points above them. So for rule-legal winners the residual blocker is
NOT player coverage and NOT legality — it is world choice plus the
law's preference for different combinations. Notably, the in-flight
all-boom S treatment solves ranks 1–200, which covers all eight best
ranks; N1c predicts the solves return different rosters, but the
boom-deep book at least enters their territory for the first time.
Their pool overlap sits mildly above chance (+0.15 to +2.19, one
negative) unlike the cohort's exactly-chance median.

   substitutes — the law should REWARD stacks so the solver chooses
   them when real, rather than construction forcing them always.

   also relax the forced 4-in-one-game concentration (it follows from
   the stack mandate; dropping the mandate drops it automatically).
