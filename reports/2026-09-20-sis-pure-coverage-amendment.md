The first frozen role/coverage gate intentionally tested a SIS coverage × receiver-alignment mechanism. Its result cannot attribute the small Brier-30 movement to SIS alone. Before any source-aware lineup shadow, run this predeclared decomposition with the same population, folds, model family, and control:

- **Pure SIS arm:** opponent prior Wide/Slot target rate, yards per coverage snap, vulnerability, and support only.
- **SIS × alignment arm:** pure SIS fields plus the existing receiver wide-share and `wide_share × (wide_rate - slot_rate)` interaction.

No FP route-shape or FP coverage features enter either arm. The result is descriptive attribution, not an adoption gate; the same support, Brier, MAE, and calibration reporting applies.
