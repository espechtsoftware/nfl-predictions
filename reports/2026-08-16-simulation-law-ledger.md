An overlap value is not portable without the design and per-arm world-sample
width. The older `54.28/80` canonical disjoint-half result used one R0 panel
with 5,000 worlds per half. It is historical context only. The current common
five-block diagnostic uses 25,000 worlds per disjoint half and measures
canonical overlap at `65.6852/80`; its 10,000-world bootstrap resamples measure
`61.1252/80`. The usable arm results are the paired same-width CBWU-OI deltas:
`-4.8148` disjoint-half and `-6.5466` bootstrap.

Any standing `54.28/80` citation in an earlier protocol or supplied review is
superseded by this width-qualified ledger. In particular, the current evidence
implies about 14 of 80 canonical slots differ across 25,000-world disjoint
halves, not 26. Historical documents are retained rather than silently
rewritten.

Experimental-law evidence is not production evidence. A transfer may be
mechanically valid while its effect fails; those dispositions must be reported
separately. The transfer tax is accepted because alternative joint laws are
legitimate research objects, but it must never be hidden.
