## 4. What it suggests (for the reviewer; nothing here is a change)
1. **Contest selection:** the FFWC qualifiers are >50% proven sharks and the $4,444 Showdown sats ~28%; the Millionaire
   satellites are ~1%. Field quality differs by an order of magnitude across the contests we enter — P1 found no measurable
   edge anywhere on money; field SHARK SHARE is a pre-lock-knowable property (entries visible at lock in the standings
   export afterwards; the regular list is built from prior weeks) that a contest-selection rule could use. A candidate
   for the study list (prereg; not a change).
2. **Construction:** the regulars' advantage is consistent with study 17 (selection redundancy) and study 18 (shape MIX):
   QB variety and a stack-shape mix near QB+2 ~40%, bring-back ~45%. Their single-player max exposure (~53%) suggests an
   entry-level player cap (study 1b's EW) is not what separates them — consistent with 1b's verdict.
3. **No evidence of private data** in the one user profiled; his picks track the field and FP-like projections.
4. **Caveats:** four weeks; the cohort is defined by volume, and volume itself buys finishing spread (we compare rates per
   entry, which removes the volume effect on counts but not on correlation); "others" is a mixed population.
