# Exact-P census review reconciliation

Date: 2026-08-15 CDT
Source reviewed: `reports/2026-08-16-exact-p-census-review.md`

## Decision

The review is right about the narrow census result and its most important
caveat. Corrected exact P was absent from the complete five-seed native union
on all 54 slates even though every P player appeared somewhere in every union.
That isolates failure to assemble the exact combination under the current
generator. Exact P is nevertheless a hindsight oracle, so distance to P is a
descriptive diagnostic and must never become a pre-lock objective or an
adoption gate. Successor construction mechanisms are evaluated on candidate
quality and, prospectively, exact-book tail performance.

Three broader claims are not supported as written.

1. Zero exact-P admission losses does not make pool admission generally dead.
   It proves only that canonical admission did not remove an already-generated
   exact-P roster. The subsequently completed CBWU-OI diagnostic changed
   admission at the same 241--265 candidate budget and improved mean candidate
   oracle C by 5.66 points, with 200/210 counts improving 8/6 to 14/10. Admission
   can therefore improve which attainable combinations survive even though it
   cannot recover a combination absent from the native union.
2. The 4.76-swap exact-P distance cannot be compared directly with the earlier
   3.46-versus-3.30 Millionaire-winner null. The targets, candidate books,
   exposures and slate population differ. That null independently sampled the
   nine winner-player marginals and explicitly did not preserve legal lineup,
   salary or stack structure. No exact-P-matched null has established that the
   current 4.76 value is undirected.
3. Static family eligibility does not prove family mix is immaterial. It says
   no family met the preregistered coarse structural-exclusion rule. Different
   eligible families can still have different unique-combination yield and C
   density. Those quantities were not estimated by the census.

## Capacity diagnostic disposition

A same-law capacity curve remains scientifically valid, but the positive
equal-budget CBWU-OI result makes it confirmatory rather than the next decision
point. It is placed behind the already-frozen ATLAS diagnostic and the paired
CBWU-OI selector-stability diagnostic.

If it is later run, it must be a nested `1x/2x/5x/10x` curve rather than one
10x endpoint. The 1x cell must byte-reproduce the current native identities.
The cleanest treatment is additional independently seeded books under the same
simulation, six-family generation and legality laws, with seed values frozen
before generation. It must report actual distinct-union yield and per-family
saturation rather than assume 5,800 unique candidates. It may measure exact-P
distance, player/pair/stack-core reach and family yield, but it may not read
candidate scores, select a favorable scale, license an arm or change
production.

The curve would measure sensitivity to more draws from the existing law. It
would not, by itself, prove that the law is directionally useful in a pre-lock
sense, because the target remains outcome-oracle P.

## Consequence

The actionable synthesis is not simply "search more." It is:

- preserve CBWU-OI as the strongest fixed-budget combination-breadth shadow;
- finish the independent ATLAS score-free falsifier unchanged;
- measure selector stability on canonical and OI pools under identical world
  resamples; and
- retain the nested same-law capacity curve as lower-priority descriptive work.

No production setting changes from this review.
