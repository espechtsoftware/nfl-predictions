The adapter is `reviews/evidence/2026-09-19-release-live-cli-proof.py`; host/fresh modes differ only in the isolated historical cache input. The actual workstation cache is never edited.

Before attributing a difference to the cache, require equal source SHA/configuration, projection batch, player identities/order, lock and salary pull, and identical content hashes for all seven live input frames. Historical cache files are separately authenticated. A changed live input, frame order or projection batch makes the pair confounded; report that fact instead of claiming a cache effect. Do not sort/rewrite source inputs to make the actual-CLI proof artificial.

Report both cache identities, row counts, changed player-frame columns, served-mean differences, and player-level mean/p95/p99 changes in the persisted incumbent and hsim selection banks. Those are descriptive distribution changes under the models. Report candidate-set overlap and ordinary selected-book overlap/order at prefixes 1/10/20/30/40/80/97. Selection banks are not independent audit banks: do not score a selection improvement on them. No actual scores, lineup efficacy, confidence intervals or new selector search belong in this inspection. No better/worse conclusion follows from membership change alone.

The prior data comparison found 3,472 historical rows differing materially in baseline inputs or activity eligibility since August 29; the feature-release drift since September 17 was much smaller and independently explained. The comparison here may establish a downstream consequence of the broader cache age, but it cannot assign three weeks of corrections to today's salary repair. If mechanics fail, retain the failed run and investigate; do not silently rerun under altered settings. Any later cache adoption requires its own explicit decision and validation trail.
