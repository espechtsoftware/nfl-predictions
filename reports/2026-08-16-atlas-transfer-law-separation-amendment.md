execution

This amendment clarifies the reporting contract of
`reports/2026-08-15-atlas-current-money-transfer-protocol.md`. The Phase S
result is known, so this amendment may not add, remove or alter an effect
criterion. It only labels already-required mechanical evidence separately and
adds non-gating law/reach diagnostics before any money-law result exists.

`mechanical_transfer_valid=true` requires every preflight, acquisition,
identity, deterministic-tie, exact-legality, finite-world and upper-bound
condition in the original protocol. If any condition fails, no effect
disposition exists and the run is invalid/inconclusive. The method and effect
must not be revised after such a failure without a separately frozen repair.

## Effect disposition

exactly the conjunction of the original three Part-A conditions:

1. aggregate mean exact legal optimum improves strictly;
2. at least three of five block means improve; and
3. aggregate q25 exact legal optimum is non-worse.

A valid failure means the Phase S effect did not transfer under the current
money law. The pre-result prior is explicitly agnostic: changing the joint
opportunity law may shrink or eliminate the ranking effect.
