but it has not. `combine_cbwu_order_invariant_books` explicitly says it is an
outcome-free research repair and is not called by production. The launched job
reads immutable R0--R4 artifacts and writes a create-only score-free report;

the registered pass logically impossible. The correct identity proof was

order-invariant; it does not pretend the treatment is inert.

`classic-k1-role12-boom40-poscal-cbwu-v4`. A passing CBWU-OI result can license
only a separately identified 2026 shadow and can supply order-invariant input
to ATLAS/exact-N. Any later proposal to promote it to the money path would first
require downstream revalidation against that explicitly changed baseline.

review arrived. No result was read while making this reconciliation, and no
