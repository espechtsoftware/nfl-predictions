# Frozen protocol: all-arms union-C census with order-statistic null (B1)

Date: 2026-08-18. Frozen before any aggregate is computed. Authorized by the
operator 2026-08-18 as a diagnostic-only census (offseason selection review
§B1 plus the reviewer-imposed null requirement). **Diagnostic label:
outcome-facing (registered `actual_score` values are read), licenses no
adoption, no selector change, no closure reversal. One execution.**

## Question

What is the realized candidate ceiling C of the union of everything ever
generated on the corrected 54-slate corpus — and how much of any union gain
is mechanical order-statistic growth versus genuine cross-arm diversity?

## Inclusion rule (mechanical, frozen)

Panels in `replay_candidates_staging` with exactly the 2023-2025 corrected
54-slate coverage (53 accepted for `20260815-atlas-money-worlds-r3-v1`,
whose 2025-W1 absence is the known recovery cell): the `20260811-lockfix-*`
and `20260811/12-pitclean-*` 54-slate arms and the complete `20260813/14/15`
five-seed families. Repair fragments, single-slate diagnostics, and all
107-slate panels are excluded (different slate/universe era; a Phase-2
protocol with full repricing would be required to admit them).

## Validity screen per roster (legality only, no strategy re-imposition)

Against the canonical corrected slate catalog
(`slate_player_features`, panel `20260815-atlas-money-worlds-r0-v1`):
nine unique ids all present in the slate; salary sum within (0, 50000];
exactly one QB and one DST; RB/WR/TE counts in {2-3}/{3-4}/{1-2} summing to
seven. Rosters failing any check drop mechanically and are reported per
panel. The $49k floor and stack rules are strategy constraints, not DK
legality, and are NOT re-imposed (they held at each roster's own
generation).

## Outputs (all reported together, one shot)

1. Union C per slate = max registered `actual_score` over valid distinct
   rosters; mean and the full 187/194/200/210/220/230/240 grid, beside the
   canonical C (181.07) and CBWU-OI C (186.73) references.
2. **Order-statistic null**: union-C growth curves C(k) for k in
   {1,2,5,10,20,all} books, R=200 uniform book subsamples per k, drawn (a)
   homogeneous — within same-generator seed families only — and (b)
   heterogeneous — across arms. The heterogeneous-minus-homogeneous gap at
   matched k, with subsample IQRs, is the diversity read; the raw union
   number may not be quoted without it.
3. Per-arm attribution of slate maxima and per-panel drop counts.

## Interpretation bounds (predeclared)

Arms were outcome-viewed at panel level, so union C is an optimistic
ceiling estimate of a prospective union policy. If union C < ~190 mean, the
194-mean target is unreachable from everything ever generated and routes to
construction/law work. If >= ~190 AND the heterogeneous gap at matched k is
materially positive, B2 (union-admission, score-blind, separately
authorized in the one-shot family) earns its slot. No threshold, panel
subset, or screen may change after the read.
