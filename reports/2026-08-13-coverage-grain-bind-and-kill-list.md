## Grain-bind rule

A coverage result closes only the exact combination of:

1. source and measurement construct;
2. source grain (player, defender, team or game);
3. target grain and position population;
4. point-in-time window and matchup crossing;
5. model location (marginal feature, dependence law or candidate rule); and
6. registered gate.

Similarity of football language is not mechanism equivalence. In particular,
team shell deployment, opponent secondary quality, current cornerback
availability and individual WR/CB assignment are four different constructs.
