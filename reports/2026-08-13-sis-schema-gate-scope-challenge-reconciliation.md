# Reconciliation: SIS schema-gate scope challenge

Date: 2026-08-13. This reconciles
`reports/2026-08-13-sis-schema-gate-scope-challenge.md` before reading any
historical SIS alignment-attempt value or lineup score.

## Decision

The challenge is correct about the material scope error. The frozen schema
screen killed one cheap team-Totals path to a **per-coverage-snap** rate. It did
not kill SIS player-grain coverage denominators, team-level attempt
composition, or conditional receiver allocation. The immutable result remains
valid; its queue consequence must be read subject to this narrower grain bind.

Conditional allocation is therefore active again as a distinct mechanism.
The next test is the separately preregistered ASOE acquisition in
`reports/2026-08-13-sis-asoe-acquisition-protocol.md`, followed by a
mean-preserving allocation/dependence gate and, only on a pass, an exact-80
score comparison.

## Corrections to the challenge

Two implementation details require correction without weakening its central
point:

1. The four manually downloaded Fantasy Points alignment files are full-season
   summaries and cannot be used for historical target weeks. The report has a
   historical Week(s) control, so the required 56 exact last-four prior-window
   exports are available now and will be collected now. This is an acquisition
   requirement, not a seasonal wait.
2. Schedule expectation should use Wide/Slot route composition, not all four
   buckets as interchangeable pass attempts. The first ASOE construction will
   normalize the defense's Wide and Slot `Att` fields against one another and
   use strictly-prior offense alignment routes only in its schedule adjustment.
   Inline is retained as a support/accounting diagnostic; Backfield is excluded
   from the WR allocation law.

The challenge is also correct that player-grain SIS remains available at a
higher cost. Costing that pull remains a separate branch if the cheaper ASOE
input fails its allocation gate; it is not dismissed by the team schema result.

## Kill-list wording

Record the completed screen as:

> Team Pass Defense Totals, Wide/Slot x Man/Zone, team/game grain: closed only
> as a source of coverage-snap-normalized efficiency because the export lacks
> coverage snaps and targets. `Att`-composition ASOE and player/defender-grain

This wording is binding for the final forensic census and supersedes any
broader reading of the original queue consequence.
