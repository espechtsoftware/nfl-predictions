## What this establishes and what remains

Restoring legitimately available Week1 information affects opportunity support and selection substantially even when projections and the pool stay fixed. This is a stronger operational case for completing repair validation than the production mean-projection delta alone. The full-feature/leakage pass and exact historical parity are reported independently in workstation handoff`b72e465`.

This does not prove better actual220+performance. Conditional MonteCarlo intervals quantify simulation randomness, not uncertainty about the true football law. Candidate generation on repaired inputs may produce a different pool; the finalD12800book is not this ThursdayD6400diagnostic. Live adoption still needs complete projection-cache provenance and reviewed operational steps.

A model issue remains visible: exact zero observed target/carry shares can give a player zero prior weight despite being activity-eligible, and multiplicative calibration cannot revive a zero. TE zero-score rows rise22→43; none of the newly zero rows is selected in either compared book. That does not justify an ad hoc floor; smoothing/participation modeling should be a separately evaluated experiment. Fixing the salary contract should not be confused with proving every downstream usage rule optimal.

No live data, policy, timer or entered lineup changed. Scratch datasets remain in use. Next: complete model/cache provenance, test the explicit live schedule-input repair, and measure the combined usage+schedule case with a separately frozen design rather than assuming additive effects.
