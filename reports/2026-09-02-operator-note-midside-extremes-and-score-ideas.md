# Operator-side note: the "mid/side" framing — can we erase the corpus middle by emphasizing extremes?

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Author:** operator-side assistant (outside review; this is neither a production nor a lab document, and it
carries no queue authority)
**Audience:** operator, lab experiment team, production implementation team
**Purpose:** answer an operator question and pass along ranked suggestions. The question, from audio
mastering: a mid/side matrix splits a stereo signal into M = L+R (the center, what both channels share) and
S = L−R (the sides, what they don't); inverting one channel's polarity and summing nulls M and leaves only S.
Can we do the analogous thing to the candidate corpus — erase the middle-scoring mass by emphasizing the
extremes?

Standing-law posture: nothing here requests interrupting 087/088, reordering the authorized queue, or opening
any outcome. Historical numbers below are development/screening evidence under the standing rules; prospective
2026 frozen books and settlement remain the confirmation and adoption authority. Where a cited figure is
outcome-mined or diagnostic-only, it is labelled as such.

## 2. Four translations of the mid/side idea, each with its status

The analogy is not one idea; it decomposes into four, and they have very different dispositions.

### 2.1 Literal — delete the middle-scoring candidates from the corpus: **falsified; spend nothing**

Removing admission 575→250 changed nothing, twice (lab reads 033/035). The selector only ever takes the top
of its own ranking; the corpus middle never reaches a book, so erasing it is a no-op. The failure is not that
the middle is audible — it is that the ranking puts the wrong lineups on top. Any version of this idea that
operates by pruning the pool (rather than changing the ranking or the law) is already answered.

### 2.2 Emphasize extremes in the selection objective: **this is the incumbent stack, and its sharpest form is reading out right now**

Coverage-194 → dual-law expected-max (adopted, ~+1.3–1.4 at K80) already places all selection weight on
extremes. The deeper, genuinely mid/side-shaped point is this: a coverage line at an **absolute** 194 rewards
the *common* component — riding a high-scoring slate — but the winning score rises in exactly those slates,
so the "M" component (shared game environment, chalk core, everything every candidate loads on) carries
little *win* information. A **winner-relative** utility cancels it by construction, the way an M/S matrix
nulls the mono sum. That is WEMAX, and it is what 087 measures on the same frozen pool.

One carried caveat: GLOBAL_WEMAX_PROXY is a pooled absolute-score screen, not same-slate win probability
(r ≈ 0.83 dependence with raw score) — so 087 tests a *partial* center-cancellation. The full version is
slate-conditional and ultimately **field-relative** (beat the realized field maximum, duplication-aware),
which is data-gated on Week-1 field/ownership/standings capture (the A6 asks). No new work is suggested here:
wait for 087, and treat the field-relative objective as the end-state this line of thinking points at.

### 2.3 Mid = consensus, sides = idiosyncratic: **the instinct has real evidence — and it is queued, not adoptable**

If "the middle" means the *consensus* mass of the corpus (what many generation routes agree on; chalk-core,
high-recurrence, high-overlap lineups), the evidence says the operator's instinct is pointed the right way:

- Multi-route rediscovery is **anti-correlated** with tail value on the lab 21k panel (194+ hits per 1000:
  1-route 3.8, 2-route 1.3, 3–4-route 0.0) — generator consensus marks ordinary lineups, not good ones.
- The legacy selector union is more concentrated than the high-score population, and the adopted selector
  under-retains the minimum-concentration `2 catcher / 1 bring-back / 4 same-game` phenotype (E0 structural
  read + outcome-disabled D800 census).
- Retrieval misses at 194+ are leverage-dominated (45/48 lev-tagged, with *lower* sim ceilings than captured
  ones — q99 188 vs 193) while modern books select lev at 3.2% with zero 210+/220+ hits vs boom's 117/30.
  (The 45/48-style figures are outcome-mined old-panel landmarks — production's caveat, accepted — they
  nominate hypotheses, they do not estimate effects.)

And the counter-evidence that forbids adopting a novelty weight today: production's R6 panel shows the
**opposite** recurrence pattern (3–4-arm recurrence denser than 1-arm; different panels/routes/thresholds,
neither invalidates the other), and the anti-consensus cells are statistically hollow at current counts
(expected hits < 1). Also already closed: plain DPP / diversity penalties as selectors (closed as
descriptors; a quality-weighted DPP challenger already runs).

**Disposition:** this translation is exactly what 088's minimum-concentration retention arm, KG-4 leverage
calibration, and KG-1A/1B are frozen to test, on proper exposure controls. The suggestion is to *support and
power those tests* (the compact R6 package + candidate trace are the blockers), not to fork a new
anti-consensus selector.

### 2.4 The audio-exact version — keep the mono sum, widen the sides: **the genuinely open, high-leverage translation**

M/S widening preserves the mono sum while boosting decorrelated content. The simulator analog: **preserve
player marginals, widen joint-tail dependence** — and unlike most analogies, the deficit here is directly
measured (the §1 co-boom numbers). The system's realized world has a *wider stereo image* than the incumbent
law simulates: correlated booms (QB+WR1, cross-game) happen roughly twice as often as the law proposes and
judges. A generator seeded by too-mono worlds cannot propose, and a judge scoring under them cannot
recognize, joint extremes at their realized rate — which is consistent with both the leverage-family
undervaluation and the ±1 ceiling on selector-only levers.

The already-specced instrument for this is untried-ideas **1.1 (ECC repair)**: walk-forward quantile-map the
sim's marginals to realized levels, reassemble worlds through the simulator's **own** rank/dependence
ordering (ensemble copula coupling), optionally recouple specific pairs toward the 003-recal realized
co-exceedance targets. Note that the ledger already encodes the mono-compatibility lesson in its own
vocabulary: replacement-form overlays that discard the sim's joint ordering lose (Schaake, system-study
Addendum 99; the replacement analog copula, −1.9), while the additive form keeping z0 is the only family that
has won (lab reads 010 vs 010a). ECC deliberately keeps the ordering — it widens the image without breaking
the mono sum. Its sibling **1.2 (min-KL world tilting to realized moments)** is the weights-only version:
keep every world, reweight so simulated co-exceedance frequencies match walk-forward realized targets, with
outcome-free effective-sample-size and book-turnover gates before any read.

Why this can beat the ±1 selector ceiling: it corrects proposer and judge at once — the lab's ranked attack
(i) — and its gates are free and outcome-blind (the frozen Schaake instruments: realized variogram score,
co-exceedance Brier, lineup-tail reliability). A null is also cleanly informative: it would say level-and-
dependence repair of this law is insufficient, which no current experiment can say.

> **Production review — recommend as the lab's next new scientific workstream, after 088.** This is the
> strongest genuinely new proposal in the note because 087 and 088 operate on an existing D800 pool, while
> a dependence repair can change both which candidates are generated and how they are judged. It needs four
> corrections before preregistration:
>
> 1. **ECC and minimum-KL tilting are different interventions.** ECC changes the proposer and judge only when
>    the recoupled worlds feed both generation and selection. Importance-weighting an already frozen bank
>    changes the judge but cannot rescue candidates the proposer never generated. Report those estimands
>    separately; if both are run, declare one family and its multiplicity control in advance.
> 2. **The scientific gates are not outcome-blind.** Variogram, Brier, co-exceedance, and tail-reliability
>    scores require realized outcomes. Fit targets on earlier seasons and evaluate once on the next held-out
>    season/slate block. “Outcome-blind” should mean target-fold outcomes are unavailable to fitting,
>    mechanics, generation, and selection until the book is frozen—not that the final evaluation has no
>    outcomes.
> 3. **Preserve a clean mechanics ledger before the score read:** marginal quantiles, rank-correlation and
>    target-pair co-exceedance movement, effective sample size and maximum world weight, delivered unique
>    candidates, exact compute, book turnover, and source/version identity. These catch a repair that wins by
>    collapsing the world bank or silently changing supply volume.
> 4. **No target/grid chasing.** Freeze a minimal ECC candidate and a minimal-KL candidate from prior-fold
>    evidence. The earlier Schaake and replacement-copula losses make a broad adaptive search especially
>    vulnerable to multiplicity. Final adoption still requires the generation × admission × retrieval
>    crossing; component gains must not be added arithmetically.

## 4. Calibration and cautions

- **What "dramatic" realistically means here.** Adopted levers in this program pay +1 to +2 each (dual-law
  EMAX +1.3–1.4; D800 volume +1.216; blend +2.2 on covered slates). The 13-point regret and the −55 mean gap
  to matched Milly winners (0/93 books have ever beaten one; median winner 234) are hindsight bounds, not
  recoverable balances. The honest route to a dramatic season delta is stacked verified +1s, the entry curve
  (item 3), and removing live leaks (item 2) — not a single silver lever.

  > **Production review:** “stacked verified +1s” means a sequence of independently useful findings, not an
  > arithmetic forecast. Gains can overlap or cancel—the cap-4 result on incumbent supply and its null on
  > boom-first supply are the recent example. Only the final generation × admission × retrieval crossing can
  > establish the combined effect.
- **Fresh cautionary tale, one day old:** the legal-relevance scheduler screened at +1.9 (all banks positive,
  W/L 48/24/0) and died on its same-week frozen confirmation (PREREG-052: raw −0.354, proxy +0.001, bank-580
  veto, all four LOSO negative). Winner's curse across a 2×2 is alive and well; every idea above goes through
  the same discipline — outcome-blind gates first, single frozen contrasts, fresh banks, bank/LOSO vetoes,
  prospective shadows as the only adoption surface.
- **No retrospective tuning** on the 72- or 107-slate panels is proposed or implied anywhere in this note.
