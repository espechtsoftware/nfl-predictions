Frozen after the read-only Week2 support census, before computing treatment market values. This is a severity/consumer diagnosis, not an outcome study, new betting policy or authorization for another live repair. Source defects were already demonstrated in synthetic preflight; now use the exact saved September18quotes to measure their current numerical effect.

Treatment: apply the existing common Sunday-main lock cutoff; for each season/week/event/bookmaker/market/rawplayer, retain the entire latest observed snapshot before that cutoff. Preserve every point and side in that snapshot, including genuine simultaneous alternate thresholds. Drop exact duplicate quote rows; refuse conflicting prices at an identical event/book/market/player/point/side/snapshot key. Do not backfill an absent side from an earlier snapshot. The existing market_points function then discards incomplete pairs and keeps its same de-vig, conversion, name matching, market averaging, minimum-two-market requirement and fallback contract.
