September 18, 2026. Erich authorized proceeding after reading the usage/renewal review.

support checks, not a performance experiment or a reason to adopt a new money policy.

1. Inspect latest registered model feature names for canonical tail_k1, role, and Route variants;
   retain object identities, exclude metric values, and compare to declared feature contracts.
2. Exercise the existing Odds consumer on synthetic moved-line, mismatched-side, partial-component,

4. Verify deployed SIS/Route shadow execution and scheduler metadata; no launch or scheduler mutation.

updates, no billing changes. Warehouse queries use a maximum-bytes cap. Reads of model metadata explicitly
omit validation metrics. Store summaries and exact query/script provenance under reports/reviews/evidence.

study. FP needs timely incremental role fields and an eligible change cohort before model fitting. SIS
needs real shadow readiness/support and a current-law transfer plan before new compute. The full live
source ablation needs exact archived inputs/cache/model identities and independent worlds; job metadata
alone cannot stand in for it. Any outcome study gets a frozen consumer, cohort, objective, cost and reader
before outcomes are opened. The previously agreed known-law E0 sequence remains in force.
