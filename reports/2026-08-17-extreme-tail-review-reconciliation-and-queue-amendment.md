## Findings accepted

1. **Portfolio-aware generation is a genuinely new construction mechanism.**
   The current generator predominantly optimizes individual lineups or
   individual worlds and only later applies portfolio selection. A pricing
   solve for the lineup's marginal value against the residual coverage of the
   current book can create complements that a static candidate pool cannot
   recover. This is the highest-priority new construction family after the
   current ATLAS and constraint-lattice boundaries close.

2. **DST is not simulated as a lineup-world random variable in production.**
   Live DST rows use `draw_idx=-1`, production disables `DST_CORR_DRAWS`, and
   `_row_draws` repeats the point projection in every world. The old scalar
   inverse-offense multiplier does not close a discrete, event-coherent DST
   model. A sacks/turnovers/touchdowns/points-allowed shadow law is a distinct
   mechanism. Its net tail effect is explicitly two-sided because a DST boom
   also represents failure by the opposing offense.

3. **The forensic ceiling is strategy-constrained.** The next forensic schema
   must separate `H_DK_legal -> H_strategy -> P -> C -> S`. DraftKings roster,
   salary, team and lock rules define legality; QB-stack, bring-back, RB/DST
   and same-team-RB rules are strategy. The new layer is descriptive and
   cannot itself promote a relaxed construction policy.

4. **The field fallback is a correctness defect.** `sample_field` retained
   the first random attempt even when it exceeded the salary cap. The local
   correction retains only salary-legal attempts, uses a bounded 32-attempt
   fill loop and drops an infeasible entry rather than emitting an illegal
   lineup. Expected dollars remains diagnostic because this does not repair
   the field law, duplication or payout model.

5. **Fixed 194 remains a comparator, not the complete objective.** New
   construction work must retain the full 187/194/200/210/220/230/240 grid and
   must use a prospectively frozen tail utility during generation. Because the
   operator values an exceptional weekly maximum over mean score, no future
   scalar utility may silently make numerous shoulder clears dominate a rare
   230/240 gain. Exact weights or lexicographic tiers must be frozen before
   treatment output exists.

6. **Selector stability must be remeasured.** Any residual-world pool must
   report exact-80 overlap under world resampling, disjoint-half stability,
   changed-slate count, pair/core reach and source attribution. Better
   attainable candidate quality alone is not sufficient evidence for a
   stable weekly book.

7. **Field legality and future field-law work are separate.** The immediate
   legality correction is unconditional. A contest-specific money objective
   remains deferred until legal field generation, duplication, tie splitting,
   contest fills and payout calibration exist.

## Deterministic queue amendment

An operational audit found that the dormant constraint-lattice, stack-core,
coherent-state and recourse watchers shared an ATLAS gate but no common heavy-
job lease. They could therefore all release when ATLAS/parity closed, violating
the one-heavy-experiment rule.

At `2026-08-17T19:20Z`, all four prospective run directories were confirmed
absent. The local stack-core support/treatment/lock/historical processes and
the coherent score-free/historical and recourse score-free processes were
terminated without cancelling or changing a Cloud Build, Cloud Run job,
execution or GCS object. Their exact launch identities remain in `HANDOFF.md`
for later reproducible resumption.

The queue is now:

1. running ATLAS repair5 -> repair6 -> historical v4 -> continuous parity;
2. already-frozen constraint-lattice control support and resource preflight;
3. residual-world portfolio column generation, once its prospective protocol,
   solver-size preflight and immutable source boundary are complete;
4. event-based DST shadow-law characterization and source/mechanics gate;
5. paused coherent-state and recourse-aware score-free mechanisms, resumed
   one at a time without overlap; and
6. parked stack-core x shell, followed by robust multi-law work.

Readiness work for later entries may proceed locally while an earlier cloud
cell runs. A later mechanism may not consume cloud compute merely because its
implementation finishes first. Any exception to this order must be a
prospective operational amendment made without reading the earlier mechanism's
treatment effect or historical score.

- the exact production-multinomial source worlds and their receipts;
- a control book, fixed unique admitted-candidate count and unchanged exact-80
  selector;
- a bounded 64--256-world pricing set chosen without realized outcomes;
- tight per-world indicator bounds derived from attainable legal score bounds,
  not a generic big-M;

  tie-breaking;

- matched-budget candidate quality, 194 no-harm, at least one registered 210+
  gain, pair/core reach and selector-stability reporting; and
- a score-free stop when the candidate endpoint does not improve.

Any simulated pass licenses only a separately frozen historical shadow and a
2026 prospective shadow. It cannot directly change the money policy.

## Event-based DST requirements

The DST shadow must model discrete scoring components and couple them to the
opposing offense/game state while preserving calibrated DST marginals. It must
report PIT calibration of 15+/20+/25+ tails, covariance with opponent QB and
catchers, own-team RB, spread and total, and the change in legal candidate
stories. It must be labeled as a new research law until frozen prospective
transfer evidence exists.
