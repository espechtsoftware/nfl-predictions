# Experimental-design and arm-interaction reconciliation

Date: 2026-08-13. This reconciles the amended
`reports/2026-08-13-experimental-design-arm-interactions.md` with the actual
arm ledger, terminal stack and currently frozen Phase R/Phase S/multi-seed
protocols. No lineup outcome was queried for this review.

## Decision

Adopt the report's corrected central recommendation: retain deterministic,
same-image causal comparisons, but add explicit channel declarations,
stage-aware ordering and bounded factorials when the scientific question is an
interaction. Do not reinterpret the historical arm ledger as a global ranking.

The original assertion that weak dependence prevents a marginal improvement
from reaching a lineup sum is retracted correctly. Weak dependence lowers the
leverage of a player-tail improvement; it does not make propagation
mathematically impossible. The amended report also correctly distinguishes a
TabPFN marginal-channel feature from a component/rank-channel feature.

The active corrected-allocation Phase R and SIS ASOE Phase S questions remain
unchanged. Their outcomes have not been read. The interaction work below is a
separate, pre-result follow-on and may not retroactively alter either frozen
decision.

## What the amendments establish

1. A feature name is not an experimental treatment. Its insertion point
   determines whether it changes a served player marginal, a rank/copula, a
   candidate objective, or some combination.
2. Most recent feature arms did not demonstrate a good served upper tail that
   was later blocked at lineup level. Where the relevant diagnostics exist,
   centre metrics improved while upper-tail calibration or loss did not.
   Copula blocking is therefore an open interaction hypothesis, not an
   explanation already established eight times.
3. Tail work must report tail-specific proper scores and reliability, not only
   MAE/CRPS or a raw exceedance rate. Raw coverage can diagnose calibration,
   but it does not measure discrimination by itself.
4. A factorial is warranted only when its factors vary distinct, named
   mechanisms and every cell needed for the contrast is actually run or exactly
   reproduced under the same terminal identity.

## Remaining factual and design corrections

### The cited failures are not one homogeneous marginal family

The amended source still calls the list “eight marginal arms,” but names nine
items. More importantly, they span different stages: the Route component arm
was primarily a rank/copula intervention after TabPFN remapping; fitted K is a
within-team allocation/dependence law; fast-role included a candidate-belief
generation mechanism; and the other feature/cache arms varied player
marginals. No pooled “eight-arm marginal failure” conclusion is valid.

### The old Route component result cannot populate a new current-stack cell

The historical Route component arm was evaluated before the current repaired
allocation, active-only cache lineage and SIS ASOE decision. Its exact-80
follow-on was a different construction: an added-candidate objective driven by
an auxiliary 30-point probability delta. It is prior evidence, not the
copula-channel cell of a new current-stack comparison.

Consequently, a valid same-feature channel screen needs one current immutable
control and two current treatments:

| cell | insertion point | score-free estimand |
|---|---|---|
| `C` | no Route feature | common reference |
| `M` | Route only in the TabPFN feature/cache contract | served-marginal tail loss and reliability |
| `R` | Route only in the component/rank contract | final-served G0/G1 dependence error |

This is a three-cell screen, not a claim that the two treatment effects are
directly comparable on one metric. `M` and `R` have channel-appropriate gates.
No exact-80 panel is licensed merely because either changes predictions.

### The G0/G1 scorecard does not specify one oracle copula

G0/G1 provides aggregate multiplicity and relationship co-exceedance cells.
Infinitely many multivariate laws share those summaries. “Impose the realized
co-exceedance structure” is therefore not yet an executable or unique oracle.
Any oracle-ceiling diagnostic must first freeze:

- the complete rank-template/copula construction;
- its calibration/evaluation split and support rules;
- which moments/cells it is guaranteed to reproduce;
- exact marginal preservation; and
- a hindsight/exploratory label that forbids historical promotion or a
  permanent kill decision.

The oracle may rank prospective hypotheses and distinguish an apparent
marginal miss from a possible leverage bottleneck. It cannot prove that a
feature is universally dead or authorize adoption on the 2023--2025 outcomes
used to define it.

### Existing panels identify only supported contrasts

The fourteen common-slate candidate panels are valuable sunk-cost evidence,
but a fixed-effects regression does not create interactions absent from its
design. Before fitting any coefficient, build the panel-by-factor matrix and
report its rank, aliased columns, support for every cell and the exact
estimable contrasts. Do not report an interaction coefficient for a factor
pair lacking crossed cells. Panel launch selection and common lineage remain
mandatory limitations.

This is outcome-mining, so it stays inside the already-frozen end-of-program
forensic analysis. It may size configuration variance and nominate 2026
prospective work; it may not revive or promote a historical arm.

## Standing experimental rules adopted now

Every new protocol must state, before its metric or gate:

1. **stage:** player marginal, rank/dependence, candidate generation,
   portfolio selection, or objective/contest;
2. **channel and estimand:** the exact distributional object the arm can
   change and the metric capable of observing it;
3. **terminal context:** incumbent panel/cache/allocation/selector/objective
   against which the local effect is measured;
4. **transfer boundary:** downstream changes that require revalidation; and
5. **interaction status:** main-effect screen, factorial cell, exploratory
   diagnostic, or prospective-only shadow.

Player-marginal screens report at least Brier/reliability at fixed 20/25/30
point events, q90/q95/q99 pinball, empirical exceedance, CRPS and point MAE,
with position, season and support diagnostics. Dependence screens preserve
sorted player marginals and use the registered G0/G1 joint-q90, variogram,
multiplicity and role-pair errors. Lineup adoption remains exact-80 and
tail-first under the operator's current utility.

## Bounded interaction program

### I0 — preserve the active frozen work

Finish corrected-allocation Phase R and the already-frozen SIS ASOE Phase S
without adding a feature, changing a gate or reading a partial result. They are
needed to establish the terminal dependence context.

### I1 — current-stack Route channel screen

After Phase S fixes its control/treatment identities, implement the three
score-free cells `C/M/R` above on the exact same player keys, folds, labels,
market blend and final-served position schedule.

- `M` appends only the four existing strictly-prior Route fields to the TabPFN
  cache feature contract. It does not add them to component models.
- `R` appends only those fields to component models and must preserve every
  sorted final player marginal. It does not add them to the TabPFN cache.
- The prior Route results are disclosed but cannot substitute for either
  current cell.

`M` advances only if it strictly improves the preregistered aggregate served
upper-tail proper-score gate with complete fold/position diagnostics. `R`
advances only if it improves a preregistered G0/G1 dependence gate without a
registered relationship regression. A failure closes only that insertion
point on the current stack.

### I2 — Route marginal x ASOE factorial, conditionally frozen now

If and only if `M` passes its score-free served-tail gate, run a same-image,
five-seed 2x2 exact-80 factorial:

| arm | Route marginal | SIS ASOE dependence |
|---|---:|---:|
| `M0D0` | off | off |
| `M1D0` | on | off |
| `M0D1` | off | on |
| `M1D1` | on | on |

Run all four cells regardless of the standalone Phase S score disposition;
that condition is fixed before the Phase S result specifically so a lost main
effect cannot hide a favorable interaction. A previously generated Phase S
cell may be reused only after exact same-image/cache/player/candidate/selected
reproduction; otherwise regenerate it. The sole Route difference is the
marginal cache contract, and the sole dependence difference is the already
frozen ASOE law and beta.

The four-cell decision uses the current five-seed exact-80 tail order
`240,230,220,210,200,194,187`, then mean, with no season or average-score veto.
Report both main effects, the difference-in-differences and all cell totals;
the winning cell, rather than a one-dimensional arm label, is the eligible
terminal research law.

If `M` fails, I2 does not run. If a mechanical ASOE failure prevents a valid
`D1` cell, I2 is blocked rather than silently substituting a different
dependence mechanism.

### I3 — downstream transfer

The existing multi-seed candidate/world factorial measures generation and
selection conditional on the Phase S-selected upstream law. It remains frozen
and valid for that estimand. If I2 later selects a different upstream
Route/ASOE cell, its production conclusion does not automatically transfer;
the same fixed four multi-seed books must be revalidated under the new cell
before deployment. No seed subset or new selector rule is licensed.

### I4 — oracle and cross-panel work at closure

Run the oracle-ceiling construction and estimable cross-panel meta-analysis
only after the historical queue freeze required by the final forensic
protocol. Their outputs feed the 2026 prospective charter, not a 2025-history
adoption.

## Consequence

The interaction critique does not justify a combinatorial sweep or relaxing
deterministic controls. It justifies a more informative unit of experiment:
channel-specific score-free screens followed by one predeclared factorial when
both the mechanism and interaction are plausible. Route Share x SIS ASOE is
the first such bounded case because its data, point-in-time contracts and
separable insertion points already exist.
