## Accepted

CBWU-OI is a material policy treatment, not an in-place production repair.
Its mean selected-book overlap with canonical CBWU is only `33.19/80`, so it
cannot inherit the incumbent forensic conclusions or money-path authorization.
Any later promotion requires downstream revalidation against the explicitly
changed baseline.

A pre-Week-1 cross-environment determinism check is also warranted. It must
rebuild canonical production CBWU from one immutable source bundle on the
validated replay image and the live serving image/runtime, then compare the
ordered candidate and exact-80 identities, support masks, p-line counts and
mean-total tie-break values byte-for-byte. This is a mechanical deployment
gate, not a selector arm and not a comparison of realized scores.

Finally, a deliberate secondary objective among truly coverage-equivalent
books is a legitimate bounded prospective idea. Recourse flexibility,
candidate overlap/effective rank and pre-lock duplication risk are possible
criteria. Such a mechanism would remain selection-layer work with the
corrected `C-S = 5.007` ceiling and cannot solve the much larger construction
gap.

## Material correction: CBWU-OI was not a floating-point-only change

The `33.19/80` result does **not** measure a numerical comparison perturbation.
The two implementations differ before the final exact-80 selector:

- canonical `combine_cbwu_books` assigns each duplicate roster to its first
  seed, applies per-seed quota/fill admission in the registered R0--R4 order,
  and only then cross-scores the admitted fixed-budget pool;
- `combine_cbwu_order_invariant_books` forms the complete distinct R0--R4
  union, symmetrically cross-scores every union roster on canonical world
  blocks, and uses the unchanged score-free 194 selector to admit a different
  fixed-budget candidate pool before the final exact-80 selection.

Thus candidate admission, candidate identities and selected identities all
change. The observed +5.019 percentage-point selected-world coverage gain is
also not an equality or float-level tolerance result. The treatment improves
coverage on all 54 slates; it is deliberately different.

The comparison with `54.28/80` disjoint-half selector overlap is therefore not
causally interpretable. That earlier diagnostic held the R0 candidates fixed
and varied only world evidence. CBWU-OI changes the candidate universe and
admission mechanism while holding source artifacts fixed. It cannot establish
that numerical noise dominates evidence, or that 47 entries were chosen from
coverage ties.

## Forensic and exact-P scope

The review's conditional warning is correct: CBWU-OI is **not**
selection-only. If promoted, it could change:

- P, because the player union of the admitted candidate pool can change;
- C, because the admitted candidate identities change;
- S, because the exact-80 selected identities change; and
- every P-to-candidate distance or loss-stage statement tied to canonical
  CBWU.

No promotion occurred. Production remains
`classic-k1-role12-boom40-poscal-cbwu-v4`, and the repair4 forensic corpus,
corrected P source and exact-P census all remain explicitly bound to canonical
R0--R4 quota/fill CBWU. The corrected exact-P census is therefore not a third
configuration and does not need to stop. It diagnoses the current production
baseline. A future CBWU-OI promotion would require a separately labeled
forensic/census rerun rather than rewriting this one.

## What is actually licensed next

The existing evidence supports two score-free diagnostics, neither a strategy
promotion:

1. the cross-environment canonical reproduction gate described above; and
2. a fixed-candidate tie census that records, at every greedy step, the number
   of candidates exactly tied on integer marginal-world count and integer
   p-line count, then checks ordered identities under row permutations with a
   stable roster-identity tie-break.

Only the second diagnostic can establish whether arbitrary ties have material
scope. A later secondary-objective treatment must be frozen separately and
must preserve exact primary coverage (including per-block protections), exact
80 entries and production legality. “Near equal” coverage is not free, and
the CBWU-OI result cannot be reused as evidence that it is.
