## Result

S source law to the adopted current-money production-multinomial law. Across
five independent seed blocks and all 54 Sunday-main slates in 2023--2025, the
mean exact legal optimum of the selected 40 worlds improves from `271.5607` to
`282.4947` (`+10.9340`). The mean within-cell q25 improves from `263.2382` to
`275.8198` (`+12.5817`).

All 270 block/slate cells improve on both mean and q25 exact legal quality.
This is unusually broad score-free evidence, but it is not a historical DFS
score, payout, ROI estimate or production promotion.

## Proxy and selection diagnostics

- The mean ATLAS-proxy versus exact-rank correlation over each selected union
  is `0.6064` (cell range `0.2343`--`0.8930`).
- Mean roster-slot-bound minus exact-solve slack is `27.0615` DK points (cell
  mean range `15.3800`--`40.2488`). This confirms that the simple upper bound
  is loose, even though it separates higher-opportunity worlds effectively.
- Paired exact-quality comparisons across 10,800 selected-world pairs are
  `7,978` ATLAS wins, `28` ties and `2,794` losses.
- Mean incumbent/ATLAS top-world overlap is only `0.3519` at top 8, `1.4333`
  at top 20 and `3.8370` at top 40. Mean exact selected-world identity overlap
  is likewise `3.8370/40` (mean Jaccard `0.0510`). The gain therefore comes
  from a materially different world set, not a reorder of nearly identical
  selections.
- Mean cutoff-tie counts are exactly `1.0` in both arms; stable-index cutoff
  handling and the exact-solve identity pass remove tie ambiguity.
