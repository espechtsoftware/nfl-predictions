fixed-budget complete-union, five-block cross-scored admission. Determine
whether that candidate-pool change closes any of the corrected exact-P to C
construction gap without scoring or promoting its selected exact-80 book.

## Mechanical reproduction gates

For every slate:

1. Reconstruct canonical CBWU in registered R0--R4 order and require exact
   ordered identity/tag parity with the immutable repair4 retained pool.
2. Reconstruct CBWU-OI under all five cyclic input rotations and require exact
   ordered candidate identity and candidate-total equality.
3. Require both admitted pools to contain exactly the frozen R0 candidate
   budget, with unique legal nine-player rosters and identical five world
   blocks.
4. Require corrected exact-P to contain one independently legal roster for
   each of the same 54 slate keys and reproduce its pinned identity receipt.

Any mechanical failure yields only `invalid-or-inconclusive` and suppresses
all outcome summaries.

## Frozen analysis

Report per slate, season and aggregate:

1. canonical and CBWU-OI candidate-pool identity overlap and complete native
   union size;
2. C, the maximum realized candidate score in each admitted pool, with counts
   at 187/194/200/210/220/230/240 and paired OI-minus-canonical differences;
3. minimum player-swap distance from corrected exact P to each admitted pool,
   including the count and identities of equally closest candidates;
4. exact-P player-slot representation in each pool; and
5. canonical versus OI structural distributions for total and positional
   salary, distinct games, largest team block, QB stack count, bring-back
   count and player-pair/stack-core reach.

Candidate counts remain fixed and equal. Complete-union size is reported only
as the discovery set from which OI admits its fixed budget; it is not treated
as submitted or admitted capacity.

Do **not** join realized player scores to the CBWU-OI selected 80, compute an OI
S layer, compare OI selected weekly maxima, or derive an adoption verdict. The
diagnostic isolates candidate admission and construction; selected-book
scoring would be a separate historical arm that is not licensed here.

## Interpretation

- Better OI C tails or smaller P distance establish a prospective
  fixed-budget construction lead and prioritize the already licensed 2026
  CBWU-OI shadow. They do not promote it or set any new weight/threshold.
- A null result says this one complete-union admission rule did not close the
  gap. It strengthens the composition/search diagnosis but does not prove
  every possible breadth mechanism is irrelevant.
- The canonical exact-P census remains a separate diagnosis of current
  production and may proceed independently. If CBWU-OI is ever promoted, the
  full P/C/S decomposition, distances and census must be rerun against the new
  baseline.
