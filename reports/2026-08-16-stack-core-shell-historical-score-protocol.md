# Stack-core x shell exact-80 historical-score protocol

Date frozen: 2026-08-16, before the stack-core/shell support census launched,
before any stack-core/shell treatment was constructed, and before any
stack-core/shell score-free effect existed.

Protocol ID: `20260816-stack-core-shell-historical-score-v1`.

## Question and consequence

If the independently frozen stack-core/shell support census licenses an anchor
and the complete five-fold score-free treatment is mechanically valid, does
the corresponding single production-form treatment improve realized weekly
maximum scores under the operator's tail-first exact-80 objective?

This mechanism was designed after the 2023--2025 outcomes had already been
viewed elsewhere in the project. Historical scoring is therefore descriptive
and can prioritize or de-prioritize its already licensed 2026 pre-lock shadow;
it cannot promote production, change a UI default, tune the mechanism, or
retroactively validate it. A score-free failure does not reach this diagnostic.

## Exact upstream gates

The scorer remains inert unless all of the following immutable inputs exist
and pass their strict finishers:

1. support run
   `20260816-stack-core-shell-control-support-census-v1`, with the first
   adequately supported anchor in the frozen `230,220,210` order;
2. score-free run `20260816-stack-core-shell-scorefree-v1`, with complete
   54-slate/270-fold mechanics and disposition
   `stack-core-shell-shadow-licensed`; and
3. the exact R0--R4 production-multinomial artifacts and repaired R3/2025 Week
   1 source already bound by the score-free protocol.

The future launcher must freeze the report and completion SHA-256 values,
object generations/sizes, exact code/image identities, 54-row accepted
execution ledger and 270 source-artifact receipts. A caller-supplied hash alone
is insufficient: the scorer and finisher independently validate the upstream
identities and dispositions.

## One production-form book per slate

For every one of the 54 Sunday-main slates in 2023--2025:

1. Reconstruct the exact five native R0--R4 candidate/player-world books.
2. Construct the full five-block order-invariant CBWU-OI control using the
   unchanged p194 admission law and the R0 candidate budget.
3. Select the control's exact 80 entries with the unchanged deterministic p194
   selector over all 50,000 worlds.
4. Rank the control parents across all five blocks with the same frozen
   worst-block/aggregate p230, p210, p194 and aggregate-mean order as the
   score-free treatment.
5. Build exactly 32 cores and 128 shells with the same four-per-QB and
   eight-per-dominant-game caps. Cross them under the same strict incumbent
   legality, retain the same 256 beam, and select the same 40 pair-expanding
   proposals. No fold result, realized score or new parameter is used.
6. Append the 40 proposals, sort by stable roster identity, reapply the same
   p194 admission law to the unchanged candidate budget, and select exactly 80
   treatment entries with the unchanged deterministic p194 selector over all
   five blocks.

Failure to fill any fixed library, beam, proposal book, candidate budget or
exact-80 book makes the historical population mechanically invalid. There is
no refill, alternate threshold, fold voting, best-fold selection, union of
five 80-entry books, or parameter fallback.

## Outcome boundary

The complete control and treatment roster identities, simulated support
receipts and source artifact hashes are locked before the scorer may query the
actual-score source. The actual source is the accepted point-in-time player
catalog's final `actual` field for the same 54 slates. It must reproduce every
native candidate's stored `actual_score` from its nine player IDs within
absolute tolerance `1e-9`, with no missing/nonfinite player score and no
duplicate player/slate row.

Actual score, contest rank, payout and post-lock ownership cannot participate
in component construction, proposal ranking, admission, selection or any
mechanical recovery decision.

but it is not the candidate-pool or selected-book result and cannot affect the
decision.

## Tail-first historical disposition

The separately labeled historical signal is positive only if the mechanically
valid treatment:

1. gains at least two aggregate exact-80 weeks at or above 200;
2. does not lose aggregate exact-80 weeks at or above 210; and
3. does not lose candidate-pool weeks at or above 200.

Report the complete high-to-low `240,230,220,210,200,194,187` comparison,
season signs and mean, but do not invent a retrospective scalar utility.
Extreme-tail tradeoffs remain disclosed and research-only. The machine
dispositions are `historical-tail-first-positive`,
`historical-tail-first-not-supported`, or `mechanically-invalid`; every one
retains `production_change_licensed=false`.
