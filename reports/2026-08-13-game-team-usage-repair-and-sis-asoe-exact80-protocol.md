# Game-team usage repair and SIS ASOE exact-80 protocol

Date frozen: 2026-08-13, after the score-free ASOE Stage A and incumbent
seed-variance reports completed, but before any lineup outcome generated by
the corrected allocation unit or ASOE final-served treatment is queried.

## Defect and mandatory repair

Season replay passes all target-season weeks to the simulator in one frame.
The finite-K usage path factorized only team abbreviation, so a franchise's
players from different games shared one season-wide target/carry allocation.
The intended and live-valid unit is `(game_id, team)`. Missing game or team
identifiers receive row-unique sentinels and cannot create synthetic sharing.

This is a correctness repair, not an optional scoring arm. Both sides of every
comparison below use the repaired unit. Existing finite-K lineup panels are
not direct controls and their scores cannot select the repaired law.

Use the terminal PIT-clean active-only stack and no unrelated change:

  `tabpfn_active_label_treatment_v2`, and 45/55 model/market blend;

  legal selected lineups per slate; and
- unchanged inputs, training, candidate solvers, selector and tiebreakers.

Use all five seed pairs R0--R4 frozen in
`reports/2026-08-13-incumbent-seed-variance-protocol.md`. No additional seed
may be added or removed after a result is seen.

## Phase R: repaired standing-law revalidation

For each seed, generate a same-image repaired multinomial arm and repaired
finite-K arm. Their sole causal difference is:

- multinomial: `GAME_SIM_USAGE` and `DIRICHLET_K` absent;
- finite K: `GAME_SIM_USAGE=dirichlet` and
  `DIRICHLET_K=28.154043586960896`.

Report the exact-80 weekly-best count at
`240,230,220,210,200,194,187` for every seed and the sum and mean across the
five seeds. The five copies of a slate are Monte Carlo replicates, not extra
historical weeks. Select the arm with the higher summed count at the first
nonzero threshold in that order. If all summed counts tie, select the higher
mean of the 270 paired weekly maxima; an exact tie retains finite K. This
expected-over-search-seed rule replaces the invalid one-seed standing-law
result and has no season veto.

The selected Phase R law becomes the repaired incumbent. Regenerate its
2019/2021/2022 portion under the matching historical terminal configuration
and repaired allocation unit, then combine that with its R0 evaluation panel
to publish a corrected 107-slate reference. That reconstruction is reporting,
not another selector.

## Phase S: fixed ASOE final-served treatment

The treatment uses only the immutable source tables and exact fitted value
from the passing Stage A result:

- `nfl_raw.fantasy_points_alignment_player_l4`;
- `nfl_raw.fantasy_points_alignment_team_l4`;
- `nfl_raw.sis_alignment_attempt_game`; and
- `beta=0.07771181538347656`.

Rebuild defense ASOE and player alignment score exactly as frozen in
`reports/2026-08-13-sis-asoe-allocation-stage-a-protocol.md`. Source windows
must remain `W-4..W-1`; ASOE is active only in Weeks 5--18 of 2023--2025.
No target-week, fantasy-point, ownership or contest result may enter the
treatment.

Within each repaired `(game_id, team)` target unit, let `p` be normalized
positive control target means. A unit is supported only when the opponent
ASOE is valid, at least two positive-mean players have supported alignment,
supported players contain at least 50% of `p`, and their scores are
nonconstant. Supported units use the frozen law

`q_i = p_i * exp(beta * (s_i - sum_j p_j*s_j)) / Z`.

Unsupported units use `q=p`. Team target total is unchanged. Carries, game
factors, touchdowns, component means and every other mechanism are unchanged.
The construction uses only positive predicted target means and PIT identity;
it must not use retrospective `was_active` to decide who participates.

The concentration branch is fixed now:

- if Phase R selects finite K, both ASOE control and treatment use the exact
  finite K;
- if Phase R selects multinomial, both omit finite K and ASOE changes only
  the normalized target centers before independent opportunity sampling.

For the treatment, generate paired control and ASOE target/reception/receiving-
yard worlds from the same seed and inputs. Form the ASOE raw world by replacing
only the control receptions/receiving-yard contribution (including the DK
100-yard bonus) with its ASOE counterpart; control rushing, passing,
receiving-TD and big-play contributions remain exact. This prevents unrelated
downstream random-number consumption from masquerading as ASOE dependence.
For each player, assign the sorted complete control raw values by the stable
rank order of that composed ASOE raw world. The treatment therefore retains
the control player's exact raw draw multiset while inheriting only the
target-allocation joint ordering. Apply the common active-only marginal
shaper, position scale and market blend afterward. Require exact row-level
marginal multiset/mean parity at every available checkpoint; only joint ranks,
candidate membership and selected membership may differ.

## Mechanical gates

Before reading any corrected or ASOE score:

1. the focused grouping regression and full repository suite pass;
2. one immutable image generates every arm, and its default/no-ASOE path
   proves deterministic parity except for the intended repair;
3. every panel has 18 slates per season, exactly 80 distinct legal selections
   per slate, complete authoritative labels and the declared seed pair;
4. arm pairs match player keys, PIT inputs, component means, market means,
   cache, schedules, salary, actuals and all nonregistered levers;
5. ASOE joined source week is always below target week, source hashes/scopes
   match Stage A, and treatment target probabilities are finite and sum to
   one; and
6. ASOE treatment and control row marginals match exactly while joint ranks
   and portfolio membership change materially on at least two slates.

Any mechanical failure may receive only an outcome-blind repair preserving
these arms, sources, beta, seeds and decision rules.

## Phase S decision and reporting

For every paired seed, report selected and pool-oracle weekly-best counts at
`240,230,220,210,200,194,187`, mean/median weekly best, candidate count,
selected-book overlap and every paired weekly delta of at least ten points.
Also report the sum and mean of each tail count across the five seeds.

At the first threshold in `240,230,220,210,200,194,187` whose summed ASOE
minus control count is nonzero, select the higher arm. If every summed count
ties, select the higher mean across all 270 paired weekly maxima; an exact tie
retains control. There is no average-score or per-season veto. Disclose how
many of the five paired seeds favor each arm and a slate-clustered paired
bootstrap as diagnostics only; they do not override the tail-first choice.
For that bootstrap, first average the five treatment-minus-control weekly-best
deltas within each of the 54 `(season, week)` clusters, then sample 54 clusters
with replacement for 2,000 resamples using seed `8,132,026`; report the mean
and percentile 95% interval. Seeds are never treated as extra historical
weeks.

If ASOE wins, it becomes the research/production conditional target law. If
it ties or loses, retain the repaired Phase R incumbent and close only this
team-ASOE law. Either result leaves the separately defined player-grain SIS
denominator branch open.
