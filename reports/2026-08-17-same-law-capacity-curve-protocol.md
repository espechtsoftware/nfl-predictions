nonterminal in seed R2, before any additional capacity-book panel, candidate

This implements the lower-priority descriptive diagnostic retained by
`reports/2026-08-16-exact-p-census-review-reconciliation.md`. It is not an
ATLAS repair, a selector arm, a score test or an adoption gate. Its purpose is
to measure how quickly the existing six-family candidate law saturates when it

## Consequence boundary

The diagnostic must not query, load, emit or rank on candidate scores,
realized player outcomes, `actual_score`, ownership, contest rank, payout or
winnings. The corrected exact-P artifact contributes only one nine-player
identity per slate; it contains no persisted outcome value or candidate score.
Distance to that identity is explicitly a hindsight-oracle description, not a
pre-lock objective.

There is no favorable-scale choice and no scientific pass/fail gate. All four
scales and every registered metric are mandatory. No result may change a
production setting, UI, money book, candidate budget, family quota, seed law
or historical disposition. Any future scored or point-in-time mechanism must
be frozen separately before its candidates or scores exist.

This protocol remains queued behind the already-active higher-priority ATLAS,
constraint-lattice, stack-core/shell, coherent-market-state, production-law
dependence and recourse chains. It may not consume the one-heavy-experiment
slot while any of those scientific executions is active.

## Mandatory curve measurements

For every slate and each of 1x, 2x, 5x and 10x, report:

1. source-book count, raw candidates, distinct canonical rosters, duplicate
   candidates, duplicate rate, newly distinct rosters versus the preceding
   cell and distinct yield per newly added book;
2. for each of the six base families, raw membership, distinct roster
   identities, newly distinct identities, marginal yield per new book and the
   slope from the preceding cell. Marginal yield is newly distinct identities
   divided by newly added books; slope is that value minus the preceding
   nested step's marginal yield (null for 1x). Multi-family candidates count in every
   applicable family and are also disclosed separately;
3. distinct player, unordered player-pair and QB-stack-core reach, plus the
   new reach at each scale. A stack core is the canonical QB ID together with
   any two same-team WR/TE IDs in that roster;
4. whether the corrected exact-P roster is present; otherwise its minimum
   replacement distance `9 - |candidate intersection P|`, the number of
   nearest identities, and how many of P's nine players, 36 unordered pairs
   and eligible QB stack cores are reachable anywhere in the cell; and
5. salary, distinct-game, maximum-game-count, QB-stack-size and bring-back
   distributions for newly distinct identities, partitioned by family.

Aggregate output must retain all 54 slate rows at every scale and report
mean, median, minimum, maximum and p10/p25/p75/p90 for count/yield/distance
metrics. Also report season summaries, the number of slates improving/tied/
worsening in minimum exact-P distance at each nested step, family saturation
curves and the ten slates with the smallest and largest distinct-yield ratios.
These rankings describe capacity only; they cannot select a scale.
