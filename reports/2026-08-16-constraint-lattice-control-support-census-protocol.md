# Constraint-lattice control-only support-census protocol

Date frozen: 2026-08-16, before any constraint-lattice Cloud execution,
treatment construction, exception solve or result existed. The ATLAS 32-GiB
full-cell preflight was nonterminal. No realized outcome or ATLAS effect was
opened to design this census.

Protocol ID: `20260816-constraint-lattice-control-support-census-v1`.

## Purpose and correction

The original constraint-lattice v1 protocol froze a p230-dependent construction
and held-out gate without first recording control support in all required cells.
That gate is incomplete under the project's standing support law. This census
repairs only that omission before treatment. It cannot generate an exception,
perform a swap, open a treatment field or license production.

## Exact population and allowed inputs

- Population: 2023--2025 Weeks 1--18, exactly 54 Sunday-main slates.
- Blocks: the exact immutable R0--R4 CBWU-OI source artifacts and candidate
  catalogs already bound by the lattice source amendment.
- Folds: five per slate. For held-out block `Rh`, reconstruct the unchanged
  four-block CBWU-OI control candidate pool and exact-80 control book using the
  original 194 line and fixed R0 candidate budget, then evaluate only that
  frozen control book on `Rh`'s 10,000 worlds.
- Allowed outputs: identity and source receipts, control entry/candidate counts,
  world count, and integer control book-maximum event counts at 194, 210, 220
  and 230. The 220 count is included solely for the predeclared support branch
  below.
- Forbidden inputs/outputs: exception cells, exception solves, candidate
  admission, treatment books, control-treatment deltas, realized scores,
  ownership, selected labels, ranks, payouts, ROI, contest results and any
  post-lock field.

All 270 slate/fold cells and all 270 exact source-artifact receipts must validate
before counts are aggregated or disclosed. Logs may emit only fold-completion
markers, never partial support counts.

## Frozen support law

For threshold `T`, define adequate held-out control support if, separately in
every one of R0--R4:

1. the 54-slate aggregate contains at least 540 control book-maximum events at
   or above `T` out of 540,000 worlds (at least 0.1%); and
2. at least 41 of 54 slates (the ceiling of 75%) contain one or more such
   events.

These requirements jointly reject a nominal aggregate driven by only a few
slates. They were fixed without seeing any control count. The census reports
all per-cell counts, five block aggregates, positive-slate counts and the
2.7-million-world global total.

Choose the first adequate threshold in the immutable order 230, 220, 210:

- **230 adequate:** disposition
  `p230-supported-original-gate-complete`; the original construction and gate
  remain unchanged.
- **230 inadequate, 220 adequate:** disposition `reanchor-required-p220`.
  Before treatment, a separate amendment and validated implementation must
  replace every p230 construction/ranking/admission/final-primary reference
  with p220 while preserving p230 as a mandatory no-decline context threshold.
- **230 and 220 inadequate, 210 adequate:** disposition
  `reanchor-required-p210`, with the same prospective replacement of the
  primary construction/gate anchor and mandatory no-decline context at 220 and
  230.
- **None adequate:** disposition `terminal-insufficient-support`; close this
  lattice design without constructing treatment.

No threshold below 210 is admissible. The p194 counts are lower-tail context
and retain the original 95% safety role; they cannot become the primary tail
anchor. There is no post-census discretion, alternate concentration rule or
support-threshold sweep.

## Execution and strict harvest

Use one immutable, create-only Cloud Run shard per slate, all on one validated
image/code identity. Each shard performs all five control-only folds. Task-level
retries remain zero. The repair-cycle bounded platform-retry law may be applied
prospectively to this census only after a separately frozen census-attempt
binding; absent that binding, any failed cell invalidates the census.

The aggregate report must bind the original lattice protocol and source
amendment, passed CBWU-OI report, exact 270-artifact source ledger, all 54 shard
generations/hashes and all execution metadata. It computes the frozen support
law exactly once. Opening this control-only report cannot alter cell definitions,
quotas, resource choice, outcome policy or the ATLAS queue. No constraint-
lattice treatment or resource preflight may launch until the report and its
disposition are strictly harvested.
