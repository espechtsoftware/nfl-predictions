volume: at the exact registered budget, does admitting from k books'
distinct-roster union — cross-scored ONLY on the production-law worlds —
raise realized candidate C beyond the k=5 baseline, or does weak-signal

## Mechanism (OI-v1 semantics, generalized book count)

Per slate: complete distinct legal roster union of the k books (B1's
legality screen) → cross-score every roster on the five production 10k
world blocks (sum of nine player rows per world; DST rows at static
projection per the law) → **admission** = the unchanged tail selector
(`select_tail_entries`, line 194.0, `SELECT_LSE=0`) taking exactly the
registered budget → **selection** = the same unchanged selector taking
exactly 80 from the admitted book. Both passes are outcome-blind.

## Endpoints (read once, after every arm's books are built)

Per arm k: realized C of the admitted book and realized S of the 80-book;
mean and full 187-240 grid; sparse-ladder utility; McNemar discordant
slates versus k=5 at 194 and 210; pair/stack-core/dominant-game reach;
per-source-panel admitted counts. Gate language: this is diagnostic
decision support; a positive k>5 result nominates a 2026 prospective
shadow design and nothing else; the 210+ no-decline guard applies to any
nomination.

## Predeclared risks

Dilution: admission ranks ~2,400 rosters/slate at k=51 on a ~0.2-signal;
C_admitted(k) may fall below k=5. Optimism: source books were
outcome-viewed at their own panel level. Both are why this is one shot,
frozen, and non-licensing.
