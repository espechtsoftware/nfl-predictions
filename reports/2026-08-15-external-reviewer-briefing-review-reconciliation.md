## Corrections to the review itself

### SIS ASOE is a second selected vendor mechanism

The statement that pass-tail is the only vendor mechanism ever to clear a gate
is false. SIS ASOE used Fantasy Points offensive alignment windows crossed with
SIS defensive alignment-attempt composition to alter within-team target-
allocation centers. It passed its score-free held-out allocation gate and its
frozen five-seed exact-80 Phase S comparison selected treatment:

- selected >=210 seed-weeks: 14 to 16;
- selected >=187 seed-weeks: 58 to 64;
- mean selected weekly best: 173.822 to 174.173 (+0.352);
- all other registered thresholds tied.

ASOE is not in the K=1 money policy, but it disproves both “only pass-tail
worked” and “vendor data has never acted in the dependence/allocation channel.”
The honest vendor summary is zero money-policy features, two selected finite-K
research mechanisms (ASOE and pass-tail), and multiple failed mechanisms.

### No SIS table is in production feature SQL

BigQuery contains 13 `fantasy_points_*` tables and three `sis_*` tables.
`fantasy_points_route_share` is joined by
`sql/features/017k_fantasy_points_route.sql` into training and inference. No
SIS table is referenced by `sql/features/*.sql`; SIS tables are consumed by
bounded Python research modules. The review's “1 of 3” SIS feature-SQL claim
should be “0 of 3.” This does not erase the valid research use of SIS.

### The proposed context fields have not already been acquired

The current SIS warehouse schemas contain broad team pass/rush/run-defense and
blocking values plus team Wide/Slot attempt composition. They do not contain
offensive personnel, formation, motion, player route, player target alignment
or player-by-coverage-shell splits. The Fantasy Points alignment tables contain
player/team Wide/Slot/Inline route shares, not 11/12 personnel or formation.

Therefore:

- a true personnel-conditioned concentration law needs new SIS acquisition;
- a receiver-specific route/alignment/coverage coupling needs new SIS
  acquisition; and
- a current-data football archetype can use alignment and usage, but cannot be
  described as a personnel/formation archetype.

### A retroactive favorable subpanel cannot rescue completed arms

The 2022--2025 coverage limitation is real and should have been priced into the
original vendor designs. It does not license rerunning completed arms with that
window as a newly primary scorecard after their broader results are known.

A genuinely new mechanism may preregister a data-availability-defined
2022--2025 score-free fit/evaluation split before reading its output. Historical
lineup scores remain descriptive only under the forensic closure; promotion
must come from outcome-unseen 2026 shadow evidence or a genuinely untouched
holdout.

## New mechanism dispositions

### 1. Receiver-specific QB coupling — highest-priority new vendor idea

Accept for bounded acquisition and a score-free protocol. It addresses the
specific current defect: QB-to-WR upper-tail lift is too low while three- and
four-player multiplicity is too high. Unlike a common Gumbel factor, a
receiver-specific conditional link can increase QB-WR1 coupling without
mechanically pulling WR2 in the same direction.

The acquisition should first prove, with a small fail-closed sample, that SIS
can return player/week route or target-alignment splits crossed with coverage
context and usable volume denominators. If it can, freeze a bounded historical
source manifest and a score-free G0/G1 gate before fitting. Mandatory metrics
must include QB-WR, QB-TE, WR-WR, RB-RB and multiplicity >=2/>=3/>=4. A pass
licenses a 2026 paired shadow, not retrospective money-policy promotion.

This is distinct from the failed G2 global factor and the failed TD-ledger
family. It must still disclose both as prior evidence and forbid tuning against
their outcomes.

### 2. Personnel-conditioned concentration — retain, but behind item 1

This is scientifically plausible because concentration, rather than marginal
means, directly controls co-boom multiplicity. It is not a free first attempt:
G3 already tested a PIT-valid group-specific K conditioned on participation
geometry and failed its Stage A likelihood/coverage gate. SIS ASOE separately
showed a small but real benefit from conditionally changing target-allocation
centers.

A future personnel-conditioned K must therefore be explicitly distinguished
from G3, use newly acquired personnel/formation context, and pass a score-free
likelihood plus dependence gate before any 2026 shadow. Do not substitute the
existing alignment table and relabel it “personnel.”

### 3. Vendor-informed football archetypes — accept as a separate v2 shadow

The existing prospective archetype v1 is already frozen and live-plumbed. Do
not mutate it. A separately named, fixed-realized-candidate-budget v2 may use
PIT-valid alignment/usage context to allocate candidates among football-
structural archetypes. It must keep the five seed blocks, exact-80 selector,
candidate count, draw evidence and money policy fixed and report infeasible,
duplicate, accepted-unique and compute receipts by generator.

This is a genuine generation-channel use of vendor data. Its definition should
wait for the corrected exact-stack P characterization so it targets the actual
missing region rather than the malformed loose-oracle region.
