# Complete pre-Week-1 Foundry strategy census

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Status:** outcome-blind scope and execution board; no new realized outcome
read informed this census
**Deadline:** every item classified `RUN BEFORE WEEK 1` must receive a
historical test, or an exact mechanical infeasibility receipt, before the first

## 1. Operator correction and architectural response

Regular-season Week 1 is not an experimentation milestone. It is the delivery
deadline. The already-frozen k=20 prospective shadow remains unchanged, but it
does not substitute for historical k=20 testing. No strategy is moved into the
season merely because its exact method contract or implementation still needs
to be written.

This census defines "on the table" as every distinct strategy family named in:

- `reports/2026-08-22-offseason-corpus-fill-and-selection-roadmap.md`;
- `reports/2026-08-24-foundry-system-big-picture-review-guide.md`;
- `reports/2026-08-24-big-picture-review-response.md`;
- `reports/2026-08-24-lead-architect-decision-on-230-extreme-tail-program.md`;
  and
- `reports/2026-08-25-pre-week1-historical-experiment-matrix.md`.

It does not reopen a method whose exact hypothesis already received a valid
historical test and permanent-close disposition. It also does not pretend that
missing historical field rows or an undefined likelihood ratio can be repaired
by naming an arm.

## 2. Two parallel clocks

The first scores and the complete offseason search are parallel deliverables:

1. **Fast score path:** finish G0, publish the accepted panel, run the frozen
   one-slate memory/runtime smoke, then release the canonical T230 laws and
   comparators. This path must not wait for every new fill method.
2. **Complete historical path:** build and run all remaining historically
   executable population, admission, schedule, and retrieval strategies on
   shared artifacts. Their books freeze before the single controlled realized
   grade.

The complete path is not a full Cartesian product. Every method first receives
a paired one-factor test against its correct control. Only the prespecified
high-value interactions receive 2 x 2 or 2 x 2 x 2 cells. This tests every
strategy without converting the Foundry into an uninterpretable combinatorial
sweep.

## 3. Evidence ladder required for every runnable strategy

Each strategy must end in one of four explicit states before Week 1:

1. `HISTORICAL_COMPLETE`: mechanics, all required historical simulated folds,
   immutable final books, and the controlled realized grade are complete.
2. `HISTORICAL_DIAGNOSTIC_COMPLETE`: the method is an oracle or diagnostic,
   has complete historical measurements, and never claimed book authority.
3. `MECHANICALLY_INFEASIBLE`: the frozen law ran but could not produce its
   required exact count/support within its frozen compute bound. The failure
   receipt is the test result; thresholds may not be weakened afterward.
4. `DATA_BLOCKED`: a named historical input or probability identity does not
   exist. This state requires the exact missing artifact/contract and a data-
   collection action, not a generic "later" label.

Implementation delay, deployment inconvenience, and lack of a method ID are
not `DATA_BLOCKED` states.

## 9. Test matrix without a combinatorial explosion

### Stage A — canonical shared selector surface

Run every retrieval law on the accepted v12 union and the four existing P x K
substrates. Selector work reuses immutable matrices and does not regenerate
lineups.

### Stage B — one-factor fill, schedule, and admission screens

For every F/G/A challenger, run:

```text
control population + control admission + R194
challenger population/admission + control R194
control population + control admission + T230
challenger population/admission + T230
```

This is the minimum A/B/C/D surface needed to distinguish supply from
conversion. Candidate generation is shared between the two retrieval columns.
K5 is the universal one-factor discovery dose. K20 remains mandatory for the
frozen F0/PB population-volume factorial and for the already-frozen k20
shadow; other K20 crosses run only when the candidate origins already exist or
the generation manifest fits the predeclared compute envelope.

### Stage C — prespecified interactions

Run these interactions even if a one-factor simulated result is unfavorable,
because they are the mechanism questions already on the table:

- all-boom x support-switched T230;
- hard-230 generation x support-switched T230;
- F2 winner-support x scenario tickets;
- F3 phenotype admission x hybrid-support retrieval; and
- deterministic mixed discovery x T230 block-supported retrieval.

### Stage D — one frozen realized grade

Every feasible exact final book enters one immutable aggregate grade catalog.
No simulated-effect screen removes a method from that catalog. Then acquire
the outcome lease once and grade all books against the governed realized
source. Report every method, not only the best.

## 10. Execution order and ownership

1. Finish and publish G0; run the real one-slate T230 benchmark.
2. Release canonical T230 scores immediately when its manifest/compute gate
   passes; do not wait for the later generation arms.
3. Freeze the complete selector and diagnostic registry.
4. Build the common lineup evidence/phenotype table and cross-fitted winner/
   high-tail models.
5. Generate F/G populations once per required origin and persist them.
6. Construct all admissions and matrices once, then select every book.
7. Publish exact infeasibility receipts for any arm that cannot meet its frozen
   count/support/compute law.
8. Freeze the aggregate grade catalog and perform one controlled grade.
9. Nominate prospective shadows while preserving the frozen k20 shadow.
10. Rehearse the Week-1 slate through legality, fallback, CSV export, telemetry,
    and post-settlement top-0.1%, top-100 and full-field collection.

Neo4j and the React visual layer consume these accepted artifacts in parallel.
They do not block score production or historical strategy execution.

## 11. Hard boundaries

- No strategy result is postponed into regular-season Week 1 if its required
  historical inputs exist.
- No method is called tested because its name appears in a manifest; it needs
  books/diagnostics or an infeasibility receipt.
- No closed Atlas, open-stack k=8, or B1 L2 27-feature individual-classifier
  hypothesis is reintroduced under a new label. This does not prohibit the
  registered individual-230 and training-maximum mechanism ablations.
- No held-out or realized label enters generation, admission, or selector fit.
- No field-relative claim is made from winner scores alone when full-field rows
  are missing.
- No approximate/biased world frequency is reported as a target-law
  probability without validated weights.
- No strategy is silently removed from the final grade because its simulated
  result was poor.

## 12. Definition of pre-Week-1 completion

The offseason Foundry search is complete only when:

1. G0 and the canonical T230 release are accepted;
2. every `RUN BEFORE WEEK 1` row has a versioned contract and implementation;
3. every required historical scope has an accepted result or exact
   infeasibility receipt;
4. all exact 4/14/80 books and oracle diagnostics are in one immutable catalog;
5. the controlled realized grade and influence report cover that whole
   catalog;
6. the frozen k20 shadow remains byte-identical; and
7. every promoted challenger has a legal Week-1 shadow/export/fallback packet.

That is the intended meaning of "before Week 1" for this project.
